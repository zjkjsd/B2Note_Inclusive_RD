This note documents an in-progress measurement of
$R(D^{\pm}) = \mathcal{B}(B^{0} \to D^{-} \tau^{+} \nu_{\tau}) /
              \mathcal{B}(B^{0} \to D^{-} \ell^{+} \nu_{\ell})$
at Belle~II, using an inclusive-tagging approach in which the signal-side
$B$ is reconstructed as $B^{0} \to D^{-} \ell^{+}$ with
$D^{-} \to K^{+} \pi^{-} \pi^{-}$, and the tag-side $B$ is inferred from
rest-of-event information rather than from an exclusive tag reconstruction.
Signal and normalisation yields are extracted from a two-dimensional binned
likelihood fit to the missing-mass squared and the scalar sum of the
$D$ and lepton centre-of-mass momenta, performed simultaneously in a
$D$-mass signal region and a $D$-mass sideband.
A preliminary Asimov-sensitivity study (Sec.~\ref{sec:results}), combining
the electron and muon channels, indicates an expected relative uncertainty
of approximately $12\%$ on the $B^{0}\to D^{-}\tau^{+}\nu_{\tau}$
normalisation for the current Run~1 plus partial Run~2 dataset; this
includes data statistics, MC statistics, and the two normalisation
systematics enabled so far (continuum, and the $D^{*}\tau\nu$/$D^{**}\tau\nu$
feed-down), and is expected to change as further systematics are added to
the fit and two open questions --- about the current template
configuration (Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:templates}) and about the generic-$B\bar{B}$
modifiers in the workspace used (Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:modifiers}) --- are
resolved.
