\section{Open Items}
\label{app:open}

This appendix consolidates the open items marked throughout the note.
Its purpose is to make visible which single input would close the most of
them, so that effort can be spent in the order that unblocks the most work.

Every item is marked in place with \texttt{AnalysisTBD} or \texttt{InProgress}
(rendered without their braces here so this sentence does not itself count
as an occurrence of either marker); nothing here is new, and nothing here is
a claim about the analysis.

\subsection{Artifacts that would close the most items}
\label{app:open:artifacts}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Add the \texttt{Toys\_pipeline} workspace pair behind the
        reported sensitivity to the repository.}
        This is the one remaining step to make Sec.~\ref{sec:results}
        independently checkable: with it, the modifier configuration of
        Fig.~\ref{fig:pyhf-config} could be read from the workspace file
        itself rather than off a plot (Appendix~\ref{app:fit:stored}).
        A related question about which categories' templates were built from
        signal-enriched versus generic MC (Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:templates}) has
        since been clarified by the analyst and does not need further
        action.
  \item \textbf{Check for BDT train/template overlap in the generic-MC
        background classes.}
        As found in review, BDT training uses a Run~1-only generic-MC pool in
        which roughly $68\%$ of the reconstructed \texttt{bkg\_continuum} and
        \texttt{bkg\_combinatorial} events are used for training, while the
        fit templates for those classes are built from the larger
        Run~1$+$Run~2 combined pool (Sec.~\ref{sec:data:overlap}); the true
        overlap fraction against that combined pool is therefore smaller than
        $68\%$ but not yet known, since no Run~2 reconstructed count exists in
        the repository.
        Per the analyst, the more fundamental question is whether this
        overlap matters at all: if the classifier's inputs and output are
        weakly correlated with the fit observables ($\mmiss$, $\pdl$) within a
        truth category, in-sample selection would not measurably bias the
        template shape even at high overlap.
        Two comparatively cheap checks would settle this ahead of a full
        disjoint re-split: comparing templates built only from the held-out
        validation split against the current ones, and the
        input/fit-observable correlation study of
        Appendix~\ref{app:mva:todo}.
  \item \textbf{Run the truth-category validation on one MC16rd file.}
        Closes the \texttt{D\_mcErrors} $> 512$ question of
        Sec.~\ref{sec:truth:closure}, the yield of the three categories
        excluded from the templates, and part of the composition table of
        Sec.~\ref{sec:truth:composition}.
        It is the cheapest item on this list.
  \item \textbf{Regenerate one workspace from the pinned commit.}
        Replaces the superseded tables of Appendix~\ref{app:fit:stored} and
        settles the channel, sample and modifier description of
        Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:modifiers}, which currently documents an obsolete
        realisation of the model.
  \item \textbf{Produce the per-category yield table} in the signal region and
        the sideband, per run period and channel.
        Closes items in Sec.~\ref{sec:truth}, Sec.~\ref{sec:fit} and
        Sec.~\ref{sec:data}.
  \item \textbf{Produce the classifier diagnostics} of
        Appendix~\ref{app:mva:todo}, in particular the correlation of each
        retained input with $\mmiss$ and $\pdl$ in the fit bins, which is the
        only quantitative evidence that the selection does not sculpt the fit
        observables.
  \item \textbf{Obtain a generic-$B\bar{B}$ fit with an interior minimum in
        the unmeasured 2-body and 4-body directions.}
        Three of the five family weights (measured-hadronic, 3-body, 5+-body)
        now have a usable MINOS width from the regenerated
        \texttt{run1+run2} fits; the 2-body and 4-body weights remain pinned
        at their lower bound in every stored fit.  Resolving these two,
        via \texttt{scripts/scan\_bbbar\_bounds.py}, is the precondition for
        the remainder of the systematic of Sec.~\ref{sec:syst:bbbar} and for
        fully constraining the family normalisations in
        Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:modifiers}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Decisions required}
\label{app:open:decisions}

The following are choices rather than measurements, and each is referenced at
the point in the note where it applies.
\begin{itemize}
  \item Whether the generic-$B\bar{B}$ normalisations are fixed or constrained
        in the signal-region fit is settled (constrained, via
        \texttt{normsys}); how the constraint width is obtained for the two
        families whose tuned weight remains pinned at a bound is not
        (Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:modifiers}, Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar:degeneracy}).
  \item The treatment of form factors and tracking efficiency is settled
        (HAMMER and a small correction respectively, both deferred as minor);
        extending the branching-fraction correction beyond the
        generic-$B\bar{B}$ families requires a new generator-level branch
        identifying the companion-$B$ decay mode for every event, which is
        not yet implemented (Sec.~\ref{sec:corrections:missing}).
  \item The prefit and postfit closure criteria, and the box-opening plan
        (Sec.~\ref{sec:validation:closure},
        Sec.~\ref{sec:validation:unblinding}).
  \item Whether mode replacement is enabled in the nominal analysis
        (Appendix~\ref{app:categories:replacement}).
  \item Whether the reconstruction-level defects recorded in
        Sec.~\ref{sec:recon:b} are corrected before the next production, and
        what is regenerated if they are.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Items that invalidate existing material if resolved}
\label{app:open:invalidating}

Two open items are not additive: resolving them changes material already
written.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{The rest-of-event track mask} (Sec.~\ref{sec:recon:b}).
        The bracketing defect is now fixed in the steering script at the
        pinned commit, but whether the samples behind any given number in
        this note were produced before or after that fix is not yet
        confirmed.
        Since $\mmiss$ is a fit observable, every ROE-derived number in this
        note should be treated as potentially predating the fix until that
        is established.
  \item \textbf{The classifier} (Sec.~\ref{sec:mva}).
        The generic-$B\bar{B}$ tuning region is defined by classifier output,
        so retraining requires re-deriving the family weights.
\end{itemize}
Work that depends on either should be sequenced after them rather than
before.
