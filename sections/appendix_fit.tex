\label{app:fit:stored}

They are the only concrete realisations of the statistical model in the
repository, so their contents are tabulated here --- with the important
caveat, established below, that they do \emph{not} correspond to the nominal
fit of Sec.~\ref{sec:fit}.

\subsection{Differences from the current generator}
\label{app:fit:superseded}

The stored workspaces were produced by an earlier version of
\texttt{create\_workspace} and differ from what the pinned commit generates in
four respects.
They are therefore \Superseded{examples only; they are not the nominal model
and no number should be taken from them}.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Channels.} Each stored file has one channel. The nominal fit
        is the simultaneous signal-region plus $D$-mass-sideband fit of
        Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:observables}, which the current generator builds as
        two channels of one workspace.
  \item \textbf{Fake-$D$.} Constrained here by a $\pm 5\%$ \texttt{normsys};
        free \texttt{normfactor} in the current generator. Note that neither
        matches the $\pm 3.5\%$ recorded in the analysis context document, so
        three different treatments exist in the record.
  \item \textbf{Continuum.} Fixed here through the parameter configuration;
        constrained by a one-sided $+15\%/-0\%$ \texttt{normsys} in the
        current generator (Sec.~\ref{sec:syst:continuum}).
  \item \textbf{Generic $B\bar{B}$.} Absent entirely from every stored
        workspace. The dominant background of Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar} has no
        template in any stored realisation of the model.
\end{enumerate}
