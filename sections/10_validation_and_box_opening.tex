\section{Validation and Box-Opening Strategy}
\label{sec:validation}

The $D$-mass signal region is blinded.
Every region in this section is a cross-check: none of them enters the
likelihood of Sec.~\ref{sec:fit}, which uses only the $D$-mass signal region
and the $D$-mass sideband.

Two of these regions require a caveat that materially affects what they can
be used to conclude.

\paragraph{The wrong-charge sample.}
It was intended as an independent validation of the tuned
generic-$B\bar{B}$ weights, on the reasoning that reversing the lepton charge
removes the signal while retaining the background.
Its background composition is however not the same as that of the tuning
region: the fraction of hadronic relative to semileptonic $B$ decays differs,
and the hadronic decay multiplicity spectrum differs as well.
Since the tuned weights are organised precisely by decay family and by
multiplicity (Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar:hierarchy}), a sample with a different
composition in those variables does not test the weights so much as test a
different mixture of them.
Agreement in the wrong-charge sample would therefore not establish that the
weights generalise, and disagreement would not establish that they fail.
\AnalysisTBD{identify a validation region whose composition in decay family
and multiplicity matches the tuning region, or determine the reweighting
needed to make the wrong-charge sample comparable.  Until then the tuned
weights have no independent validation, which is the principal open item of
Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar}}

\paragraph{The $D$-mass sideband.}
Because it is a fit channel, agreement there after the fit is a
self-consistency check rather than a prediction, and should not be presented
as validation of the fake-$D$ model.

\subsection{Known open issues}
\label{sec:validation:issues}

The following are known and unresolved at the pinned commit.
\begin{itemize}
  \item $E_{\mathrm{cms}}$ and experiment-level data/MC discrepancies in
        several Run~1 and Run~2 experiments, hypothesised to originate from
        background-overlay run mismatches.
  \item A fixed, non-run-dependent $B\bar{B}$ cross section in MC16rd despite
        the $E_{\mathrm{cms}}$ drift across the data-taking period.
  \item Slow-$\pi^{0}$ efficiency corrections for the $D^{*}$ veto region,
        pending an updated MC16rd table.
\end{itemize}
