\section{Decay-Category Definitions}
\label{app:categories}

\subsection{Truth categories and their integer codes}
\label{app:categories:codes}

The categories of Sec.~\ref{sec:truth} are stamped with the integer codes of
\texttt{utilities.DecayMode\_new}, which are the interface between
classification, template construction and the workspace.
The codes are not contiguous with respect to the categories actually used:
codes 15 and 16 (\texttt{gap\_pi}, \texttt{gap\_eta}) are defined but never
assigned, because the two gap modes are merged into code 17 before the
dictionary is returned.

\subsection{PDG-code groupings}
\label{app:categories:pdg}

The charm states are grouped as follows, with both charge states in each list:
\begin{description}
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ charged] $\pm411$, $\pm413$
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ neutral] $\pm421$, $\pm423$
  \item[$D^{**}$ narrow] $\pm10413$ ($D_{1}$), $\pm10423$ ($D_{1}^{0}$),
        $\pm415$ ($D_{2}^{*}$), $\pm425$ ($D_{2}^{*0}$)
  \item[$D^{**}$ broad] $\pm10411$ ($D_{0}^{*}$), $\pm10421$ ($D_{0}^{*0}$),
        $\pm20413$ ($D_{1}'$), $\pm20423$ ($D_{1}'^{0}$)
  \item[$D_{s}$ family] $\pm431$, $\pm433$, $\pm10431$, $\pm20433$,
        $\pm10433$, $\pm435$
  \item[Charm baryons] $\Lambda_{c}$, $\Sigma_{c}$, $\Xi_{c}$, $\Omega_{c}$
  \item[Double charm] $\psi$ states, $\Xi_{cc}$, $\Omega_{cc}$
\end{description}
The narrow/broad $D^{**}$ split follows the standard division into the two
$j_{q} = 3/2$ states, which are narrow, and the two $j_{q} = 1/2$ states,
which are broad.
It is used at classification time and then undone when the templates are
built (Sec.~\ref{sec:truth:merging}).

\subsection{Generic-$B\bar{B}$ families}
\label{app:categories:bbbar}

Each generic-$B\bar{B}$ candidate is assigned a decay-mode identifier from the
product of the generated daughter PDG codes of the relevant $B$, and placed in
exactly one family: \texttt{BBbar\_semileptonic} if any daughter is a lepton;
otherwise \texttt{BBbar\_measured\_hadronic} if the identifier appears in the
measured-mode list; otherwise \texttt{BBbar\_unmeasured:$n$-body}, with $n$ the
generated daughter multiplicity clipped below at 2 and capped at $5+$.

The measured-mode list is organised into four blocks --- single-charm and
double-charm, each for mixed and charged $B$ --- and contains 75 active
entries, with a further 48 entries commented out.

Because a generated decay may contain a radiated photon, the list is extended

as measured rather than falling into an unmeasured $n$-body family.

\subsection{Branching-fraction corrections}
\label{app:categories:bf}

Four measured modes carry an additional branching-fraction correction, the
ratio of the PDG value to the value used in the simulation.
All four are $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+}$ modes, and all four corrections are large.

\subsection{Mode replacement}
\label{app:categories:replacement}

An optional many-to-one map merges ten configured non-resonant modes into
resonant ones before tuning, preserving the total yield of the affected modes
by rescaling the surviving mode by
$(N_{\mathrm{old}} + N_{\mathrm{new}})/N_{\mathrm{new}}$.
The pattern is consistent: a $D_{s}^{(*)}$ recoiling against a non-resonant
$D + n\pi$ system is replaced by the same $D_{s}^{(*)}$ recoiling against the
$D^{**}$ resonance of matching multiplicity and charge, for example
$D_{s} D \pi \pi \to D_{s} D_{1}$ and $D_{s} D \pi^{0} \to D_{s} D_{2}^{*}$.
The intent is to move strength that the generator produces non-resonantly into
the resonant states that are actually observed.

Mode replacement is \emph{disabled} in every result shown in this note
(\texttt{replaceFalse}).
