\section{Decay-Category Definitions}
\label{app:categories}


The integer codes used for 
classification and template construction are given below.

\subsection{PDG-code groupings}
\label{app:categories:pdg}

The charm states are grouped as follows, with both charge states in each list:
\begin{description}
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ charged] $\pm411$, $\pm413$
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ neutral] $\pm421$, $\pm423$
  \item[$D^{**}$ narrow] $\pm10413$ ($D_{1}$), $\pm10423$ ($D_{1}^{0}$),
        $\pm415$ ($D_{2}^{*}$), $\pm425$ ($D_{2}^{*0}$)
  \item[$D^{**}$ broad] $\pm10411$ ($D_{0}^{*}$), $\pm10421$ ($D_{0}^{*0}$),
        $\pm20413$ ($D_{1}'$), $\pm20423$ ($D_{1}'^{0}$)
  \item[$D_{s}$ family] $\pm431$, $\pm433$, $\pm10431$, $\pm20433$,
        $\pm10433$, $\pm435$
  \item[Charm baryons] $\Lambda_{c}$, $\Sigma_{c}$, $\Xi_{c}$, $\Omega_{c}$
  \item[Double charm] $\psi$ states, $\Xi_{cc}$, $\Omega_{cc}$
\end{description}
The narrow/broad $D^{**}$ split follows the standard division into the two
$j_{q} = 3/2$ states, which are narrow, and the two $j_{q} = 1/2$ states,
which are broad.

\subsection{Measured generic-$B\bar{B}$ decay modes}
\label{app:categories:bbbar}

The family hierarchy is described in Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar:hierarchy}.
The measured-mode list (\texttt{measured\_pdg\_list} in
\texttt{utilities.py}) was built by examining the most populated generated
decay modes in the MC sample at generator level, each identified by the
product of its daughter PDG codes.
Each of these modes was checked against the latest published PDG listing: the
75 modes with a measured branching fraction are active entries in the list,
while the 48 modes without one are commented out and are therefore classified
as \texttt{BBbar\_unmeasured:$n$-body}.
The list is organized into four blocks: single-charm and double-charm, each
for mixed and charged $B$.


Because a generated decay may contain a radiated photon, the list is extended
by \texttt{measured\_pdg\_rad}, each entry multiplied by the photon PDG code
22, so that the radiative counterpart of a measured mode is still recognized
as measured rather than being classified as an unmeasured $n$-body family.

\subsection{Branching-fraction corrections}
\label{app:categories:bf}

Four measured modes carry an additional branching-fraction correction, the
ratio of the PDG value to the value used in the simulation.
All four are $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+}$ modes, and all four corrections are large.

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Branching-fraction corrections applied to measured
  generic-$B\bar{B}$ modes, from \texttt{bbbar\_reweighting.PDG\_CORRECTIONS}.}
  \label{tab:bf-corrections}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{l c c}
    \hline
    Mode & Ratio (PDG / generator) & Reduction \\
    \hline
    $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+} D^{-}$        & 0.142 & $\times 7.0$ \\
    $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+} D^{*-}$       & 0.085 & $\times 11.8$ \\
    $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+} \bar{D}^{0}$  & 0.104 & $\times 9.6$ \\
    $D_{s0}^{*}(2317)^{+} \bar{D}^{*0}$ & 0.053 & $\times 19.0$ \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

These reduce the simulated rates by factors of 7 to 19, so they are not small
adjustments: the generator decay file had typographical errors. This has been confirmed 
by other Belle II analyses.
