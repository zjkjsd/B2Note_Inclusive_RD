\section{Decay-Category Definitions}
\label{app:categories}

\sloppy

\subsection{Truth categories and their integer codes}
\label{app:categories:codes}

The categories of Sec.~\ref{sec:truth} are stamped with the integer codes of
\texttt{utilities.DecayMode\_new}, which are the interface between
classification, template construction and the workspace.
The codes are not contiguous with respect to the categories actually used:
codes 15 and 16 (\texttt{gap\_pi}, \texttt{gap\_eta}) are defined but never
assigned, because the two gap modes are merged into code 17 before the
dictionary is returned.

\subsection{PDG-code groupings}
\label{app:categories:pdg}

The charm states are grouped as follows, with both charge states in each
list:
\begin{description}
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ charged] $\pm411$, $\pm413$
  \item[$D$, $D^{*}$ neutral] $\pm421$, $\pm423$
  \item[$D^{**}$ narrow] $\pm10413$, $\pm10423$, $\pm415$, $\pm425$
  \item[$D^{**}$ broad] $\pm10411$, $\pm10421$, $\pm20413$, $\pm20423$
  \item[$D_{s}$ family] $\pm431$, $\pm433$, $\pm10431$, $\pm20433$,
        $\pm10433$, $\pm435$
  \item[Charm baryons] $\Lambda_{c}$, $\Sigma_{c}$, $\Xi_{c}$, $\Omega_{c}$
  \item[Double charm] $\psi$ states, $\Xi_{cc}$, $\Omega_{cc}$
\end{description}
The narrow/broad $D^{**}$ split is used at classification time and then
undone when the templates are built (Sec.~\ref{sec:truth:merging}).

\subsection{Generic-$B\bar{B}$ families}
\label{app:categories:bbbar}

Each generic-$B\bar{B}$ candidate is assigned a decay-mode identifier from
the product of the generated daughter PDG codes of the relevant $B$, and
placed in exactly one family:
\texttt{BBbar\_semileptonic} if any daughter is a lepton;
otherwise \texttt{BBbar\_measured\_hadronic} if the identifier appears in the
measured-mode list;
otherwise \texttt{BBbar\_unmeasured:$n$-body}, with $n$ the generated
daughter multiplicity clipped below at 2 and capped at $5+$.

Four measured modes carry an additional branching-fraction correction, the
ratio of the PDG value to the value used in the simulation, applied to
$D_{s1}(2536)$ modes.

\subsection{Mode replacement}
\label{app:categories:replacement}

An optional many-to-one map merges configured non-resonant 3- and 4-body
modes into resonant 2-body states before tuning, preserving the total yield
of the affected modes by rescaling the surviving mode by
$(N_{\mathrm{old}} + N_{\mathrm{new}})/N_{\mathrm{new}}$.
It is disabled in all results shown in this note.
