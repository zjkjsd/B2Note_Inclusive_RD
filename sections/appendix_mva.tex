\subsection{Data/MC comparison of the BDT inputs and output}
\label{app:mva:datamc}

This subsection collects the data/MC comparisons of the BDT input variables
not shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:mva-datamc-main}. The sample, selection and
plotting conventions are the same (Sec.~\ref{sec:mva:validation}).
Figures~\ref{fig:mva-datamc-used-cs} and~\ref{fig:mva-datamc-used-db} show
the remaining 13 variables used in training, and
Fig.~\ref{fig:mva-datamc-dropped} shows the remaining 9 variables dropped
because of mismodeling.
Most of the dropped variables, in particular \texttt{thrustOm},
\texttt{KSFWV14}, \texttt{KSFWV16} and \texttt{D\_vtxReChi2}, have residuals
that change sign across the distribution, which indicates a mismodeled shape.
For the vertex-fit quality variables \texttt{D\_vtxReChi2} and
\texttt{B0\_vtxReChi2}, the simulation underestimates the population at very
small $\chi^{2}$.

Figure~\ref{fig:mva-datamc-sigprob} shows the classifier output
\texttt{sig\_prob} in the same region with the additional requirement
$\texttt{sig\_prob} > 0.5$.
Data and simulation agree considerably better than for most individual inputs
(reduced $\chi^{2} \approx 5$).
The residuals show a mild deficit of data at
$0.8 \lesssim \texttt{sig\_prob} \lesssim 0.93$ and an excess in the last bin.
