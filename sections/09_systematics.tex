\subsection{Generic-$B\bar{B}$ composition}
\label{sec:syst:bbbar}
The treatment is to propagate the covariance of the tuned family weights
(Sec.~\ref{sec:bbbar}) into the fit as \texttt{normsys} constraints on the
generic-$B\bar{B}$ normalizations, replacing the free normalization factors
of the current workspace (Sec.~\ref{sec:fit:modifiers}).
Because the family weights are strongly correlated, assigning one independent
constrained parameter per family would discard that correlation.
The intended construction is instead to diagonalize the covariance matrix and
use its eigenvectors as a small set of uncorrelated nuisance parameters, each
varying all five family weights coherently by
$\sqrt{\lambda_{i}}\, \hat{v}_{i}$.

\subsection{$D^{**}\ell\nu$ and gap-mode modelling}
\label{sec:syst:gap}
This could be one of the large
systematics. The gap-mode template
is built from the $D^{**}\ell\nu$ (broad) shape, with the two components'
normalizations summed, rather than from the generic-MC gap-mode models
themselves.

Four related Belle~II semileptonic-tag notes examined treat this
differently enough that no single precedent is obviously the right one to
follow here:
\begin{itemize}
  \item one uses dedicated gap-mode signal MC generated via an intermediate
        broad $D^{**}$ resonance (the same substitution used here, but from
        purpose-built MC rather than a post-hoc template swap), assigns a
        $100\%$ uncertainty to the combined gap-mode branching fraction
        (varying all of them together, ``since their true composition is
        uncertain''), and separately varies the broad-$D^{**}$ form-factor
        parameters for the gap-mode decays;
  \item a second instead models the gap as dominated by
        $B\to D^{(*)}\eta\ell\nu$ rather than $D^{(*)}\pi\pi\ell\nu$, and
        assigns separate large uncertainties to the two hypotheses;
  \item a third takes light-lepton gap-mode branching fractions from HFLAV
        averages and derives semitauonic gap-mode branching fractions (not
        available from HFLAV) from predicted $R(H_{c})$ ratios
        \cite{Bernlochner:2023eqn};
  \item a fourth treats gap modes as ordinary $B\to D^{**}\ell\nu$ decays
        for form-factor reweighting purposes (the same choice made here),
        and separately notes that some phase-space-generated non-resonant
        modes in generic MC are removed because their measured branching
        fractions are already saturated by resonant $D^{**}$ production.
\end{itemize}
